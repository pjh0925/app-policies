Contact

Questions, bug reports, or ideas are always welcome. Email pjh0925@me.com, or tap Settings › Send Feedback in the app. The in-app button adds your app and OS version automatically, which helps us fix issues faster. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my diary saved?

Only on your device. There is no account, and the app works fully offline. We can't see your paintings or notes.

How do I move my diary to a new phone?

On your old phone, go to Settings › Backup › Export Backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or email. On the new phone, install Palette Diary and choose Settings › Backup › Import Backup, then pick that file.

What happens if a day already exists when I import?

For days that exist both in the file and on your device, the one edited more recently is kept. Importing the same file more than once is safe.

Can I paint or edit a past day?

Yes. Tap any past date in the Calendar to paint it or edit it. Future dates can't be filled in yet.

Can I make the replay faster or turn it off?

Yes. In Settings › Appearance › Replay When Opening, choose Normal, Fast, or Off. When it's off, you can still tap Replay on any day to watch it.

How many feelings and how long a note can I add?

Up to 3 feelings and a one-line note of up to 100 characters per day. Both are optional.

How do I change the theme or language?

Choose System, Light, or Dark in Settings › Appearance. The app language follows your device language (English or Korean).

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings › Data › Delete All Data. This can't be undone, so export a backup first if you might want your diary later. Deleting the app also removes its data from your device.
